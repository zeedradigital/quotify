Help

Quotes
Make and send a quote
  1. Enter the customer's name, phone and area.
  2. Choose From EB Bill and type the bi-monthly units or bill amount, or choose Enter kW Size.
  3. Pick the building type and system type, then tap Generate Quotation.
  4. Tap the Share button: the PDF is attached. Pick WhatsApp, then the customer. New customer not in your contacts? Save their number in the phone first. Tap Save to keep the quote in My Quotes.
Find a quote and change its status
  1. Open My Quotes from the menu. Search by customer name or mobile number.
  2. Tap the status on a quote (for example DRAFT →) and choose Sent, Accepted, Closed or Rejected. Every change is recorded with who and when.
Revise a quote
  1. In My Quotes, tap Revise on the quote.
  2. Change what you need, then Generate and Save. It is saved as a new version (R1, R2…); the earlier version stays and opens with View PDF.
Set your rates
  1. Open Price Settings from the menu.
  2. Update panel, inverter and component prices, then tap Save. Rates are saved on this phone.
For the person managing the team
First time: create the admin PIN and add staff
  1. Tap Set up your team on the quote screen, or Admin View in the menu.
  2. Create a 4-digit admin PIN and enter it twice. Keep it private; it opens Admin View.
  3. In the Staff box, type each salesman's name and a 4-digit PIN for them, and tap + Add Staff.
See every quote and the monthly report
  1. Open Admin View and enter the admin PIN.
  2. See each salesman's quotes and win rate. Filter by staff or date, or tap Calendar.
  3. Pick a month and tap PDF or Excel to download the monthly report.
Remove a staff member
  1. In Admin View, tap Remove next to their name. They can no longer log in; their old quotes keep their name.
  2. Tap Restore to bring them back.
Change the admin PIN
  1. Open Admin View, tap Change admin PIN in the Staff box.
  2. Enter the current PIN, then the new PIN twice.
  3. Forgot the PIN? Call Zeedra to reset it.
For salesmen
Site visits: see, check in and get reminders
  1. Booking a visit needs the customer's location: ask them to send their location on WhatsApp, copy it and paste it in Customer's location. Tap Open in Maps to check it's the right house.
  2. Open the app every day. A red dot on the menu shows new visits assigned to you; the banner at the top shows today's and tomorrow's visits.
  3. Open Visits to see your list. At the customer's place tap Check in and allow location.
  4. Tap Add to Google Calendar on a visit, then Save, so your phone reminds you.
  5. One-time setup for reminders 1 day and 1 hour before: open the Google Calendar app → ☰ menu → Settings → your email → Events → Default notification → choose 1 day before and 1 hour before.
Log in and switch staff
  1. When the app opens, choose your name and enter your 4-digit PIN.
  2. Sharing a phone? Use Switch Staff in the menu.
  3. Your quotes follow you on any phone once you log in.
Let colleagues edit your quotes
  1. Open Price Settings; at the top, switch on Allow others to edit my quotes.
  2. When it is off, only the admin can edit them. Quotes stay credited to you either way.
See the team's quotes
  1. Open Team Quotes from the menu to see everyone's quotes (phone numbers stay hidden).
